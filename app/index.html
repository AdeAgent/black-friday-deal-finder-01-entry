How these deals were checked

Every price on this page was read on 27 November 2026 from a deals source that names the store — a store roundup (Mashable Black Friday ads), a same-day news report (TradingView/Benzinga), a dated deals diary, or a retailer roundup (DealNews, Tom’s Guide, Yahoo, CNET). Each deal card names its source and the evidence behind its verdict. Prices move all day on Black Friday; confirm the price at the store before you pay.

The verdict on each deal is honest on purpose: New low means the lowest price the sources have ever recorded for that product. Strong price means a large cut on a real usual price. Routine sale means a modest or frequently repeated cut — worth buying only if you wanted it anyway. Where a store lists a “comparable value” instead of a past price, the card says so next to the usual price.

Deal Score (0–100) weighs the discount most, then the verdict, then the cash saved. Store links go to the store’s own search for the exact product. This site loads nothing from the network and stores nothing about you.
